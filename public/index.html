    Glossary
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Interchange
    The fee the merchant's side pays the cardholder's bank (the issuer) on each card payment.
    
        
    Card network fees (assessments)
    Fees charged by Visa, Mastercard or Amex for using their network.
    
        
    Processor markup
    The payment processor's own margin on top of the other fees.
    
        
    Interchange++ (IC++)
    A pricing model where interchange and network fees are passed to the merchant at cost, plus a visible processor markup.
    
        
    Blended flat rate
    One simple rate for every payment, for example 2.90% + $0.30.
    
        
    Tiered pricing (qualified / non-qualified)
    Payments are grouped into price tiers, with higher rates for riskier payment types.
    
        
    MDR (merchant discount rate)
    The total percentage of a sale that the merchant pays in fees.
    
        
    Basis point (bps)
    One hundredth of a percent. 100 bps = 1.00%.
    
        
    Cross-border
    The card was issued in a different country from the merchant. This usually costs more.
    
        
    3-D Secure (3DS)
    An extra online check of the cardholder's identity that can reduce fees and fraud.
    
        
    Durbin Amendment
    A US rule that caps interchange on debit cards from large banks.
    
        
    Acceptance criteria
    The conditions a feature must meet to be considered finished and correct.
    
        
    Given / When / Then
    A plain format for writing test scenarios: the starting point, the action, and the expected result.
